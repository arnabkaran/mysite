RESEARCH

Research Interests

My research focuses on the synthesis, characterization, and performance optimization of halide perovskites and lead-free perovskites for photovoltaic and photodetection applications. I also investigate the role of nanoparticles and carbon nanotubes in enhancing the electrical and photovoltaic properties of organic semiconductor devices.

Projects & Mentorship

1. To design and develop a low-cost Arduino-based digital clock capable of measuring and displaying elapsed time with millisecond resolution using microcontroller timer functions and a digital display driver

An Arduino-Based Digital Millisecond Clock is an embedded system designed to measure and display time with millisecond-level resolution. It uses an Arduino microcontroller, digital display modules, and programmed interrupts to achieve high-precision timing, making it suitable for laboratory experiments and time-sensitive measurements.

2. Arduino Based Automatic Waterlevel Controller

An Arduino-Based Automatic Water Level Controller is an embedded system designed to monitor and automatically control the water level in a tank. It uses an Arduino microcontroller with water level sensors (ultrasonic or float switches) to manage pump operation, preventing tank overflow and ensuring consistent water supply.

3. Measurement of Distance using Ultrasonic Transducer With Arduino Programming

This project demonstrates the measurement of distance using an ultrasonic transducer interfaced with an Arduino microcontroller. The ultrasonic sensor emits high-frequency sound waves and detects their return time, enabling precise non-contact distance measurement for robotics and automation applications.

4. Designed an website for One Day Nation Seminar On Skill Enhancement At Jadavpur University Department of Physics

Designed and developed a website for the One-Day National Seminar on Skill Enhancement organized by the Department of Physics, Jadavpur University, providing an informative and user-friendly platform for event registration and information dissemination.
